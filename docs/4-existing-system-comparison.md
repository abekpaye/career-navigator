# Existing System Comparison

Five platforms were selected because they cover different parts of career guidance, CV analysis and professional development. They serve different purposes, but comparing them against the same nine criteria helps identify useful features and remaining gaps.

## Existing Systems Overview

**roadmap.sh** provides learning roadmaps for developers. Users can follow a role-based path, track completed topics and create custom or AI-generated roadmaps. Its Teams product also supports skill gap analysis, but the main focus is learning rather than career matching from a CV.

**Google Career Dreamer** uses information about a person's background, skills and interests to suggest possible career paths. It highlights transferable skills and links users to career and job information. The service uses US labour-market data and is available only in the US.

**Jobscan** compares an uploaded resume with a specific job description. It calculates a match rate, identifies missing skills and keywords, and suggests improvements. Its score reflects one job application, not overall suitability for a profession.

**CareerVillage Coach** is an AI career guidance tool offering career exploration, resume feedback, skill development activities and interview preparation. Users can share their goals and optionally upload a CV. It also provides personalized advice on career steps.

**Mansap Kompasy** is a career guidance platform focused on Kazakhstan. It provides information about professions, salaries, education and regional labour-market demand. Its career orientation test helps users explore suitable directions based on their interests.

## Comparative Analysis

These platforms solve related but different problems. Jobscan measures how well a resume matches one vacancy, while Google Career Dreamer and Mansap Kompasy help people explore professions. roadmap.sh focuses on learning plans and progress, whereas CareerVillage Coach combines career advice with development activities.

Several important features already exist, including personalized guidance, roadmaps and information about Kazakhstan's labour market. The main opportunity is therefore not to introduce these features for the first time, but to connect profile analysis, occupation matching and development planning in one consistent process.

## Identified Gap and Proposed Solution

The proposed platform will let users enter their skills, languages, projects and experience manually or through a CV. It will recommend up to ten IT professions, explain a match percentage based on defined requirements and show which skills are not yet confirmed in the profile.

Users will then choose a profession, either from the recommendations or independently, and receive an ordered development roadmap. It will cover relevant skills, projects, language development and preparation for internships, with progress tracking. Kazakhstan labour-market information will be considered where reliable data is available. The value of the proposed system will depend on the quality and usefulness of these connected recommendations, which will need to be evaluated.
